Background music

Upload an MP3 / M4A / WAV or grab the audio of a link, then add it to all clips here, or tick "Background music" on a clip.

100% = as loud as the speech; 40% is a clearly audible background. Tick Mute original audio to drop the clip's own sound and play only the music (turn the volume up for those). The music loops if it is shorter than the clip and fades out at the end. Only the audio is redone, so this takes a second or two per clip. Only use music you're allowed to use.

Colour adjustment — all clips

Applies the same gentle grade to every ready clip; each one re-renders (slower than music, since the video is re-encoded) and keeps its background music. Small values look most natural. You can still set a different grade per clip on its card.
